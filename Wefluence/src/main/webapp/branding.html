Frequently Asked Questions

How do brands sign up and get started on Wefluence?

Brands can sign up on Wefluence by creating a business profile, specifying their target audience, and setting campaign objectives. Once registered, they can browse influencer profiles and initiate collaborations.

How does Wefluence handle payments for brand collaborations?

Payments are processed securely through Wefluence's platform. Brands can choose flexible payment options, including milestone-based payouts, to ensure smooth transactions and accountability for both parties.

How does Wefluence ensure influencer authenticity?

Wefluence verifies influencer profiles, analyzes engagement metrics, and provides real-time data to help brands make informed collaboration decisions.

Can brands track campaign performance?

Yes, Wefluence provides analytics and performance reports, including reach, engagement, and conversion rates, to help brands measure campaign success.

What types of influencer collaborations can brands initiate?

Brands can launch sponsored posts, product reviews, affiliate partnerships, ambassador programs, and influencer-led marketing campaigns.
